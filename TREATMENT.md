# What Happened to Youngstown? The Day the Steel Mills Closed — treatment

Episode 6 · 21:20 · narrator: AI33 / ElevenLabs "Storyteller Oxley" · 1920×1080, 30 fps · series look kept (dark warm-brown ground, IBM Plex, Source Serif italic quotes,
red only for dates and numbers, 47 px captions, mono source tags, the series foley). **New this time: the story is told as a court case.**

**Shape (the plan's):** the charge (Black Monday, 19 Sept 1977) → Mill Town title → nine parts on the docket of *Local 1330 v. U.S. Steel*:
Opening Statement · Exhibit A (what the town built) · Exhibit B (the merger) · The Witnesses (the Ecumenical Coalition) · Exhibit C (the promise, the hot line,
the occupation) · The Trial · The Defence (U.S. Steel's case, fairly, with its own figures) · The Verdict (the judges' own words) · The Jury (today, WARN, the question).
Never tells the viewer who was right.

**Motion graphics (new):** case caption card per part (a pleading's caption box types itself — real case no. C 79-2337 Y — with a ten-part docket bar);
exhibit stickers (plaintiffs' yellow, defendant's blue, as in a courtroom); witness cards (a worker's words appear in silence — the narrator never speaks as a worker);
court-record pages with line numbers that type themselves, key phrases underlined in red (the hot-line messages, Roderick's testimony, Lambros's words, the Sixth Circuit);
the hot-line handset with a recording level; the Save Our Valley passbook counting to $4,014,927; the scales of justice (the same 1980 figures read two ways balance level);
a Mahoning River map drawn from coordinates with the four mills lighting and going dark; 10,000 jobs as dots falling in three waves; the census line; an empty jury box.

**Archive:** *Steel Town* (OWI, 1944, National Archives) — Youngstown's mills, workers, homes, orchestra, gate; National Archives 1918 Sheet & Tube photos; Library of
Congress panoramas (1882, 1905, c. 1910); Alfred T. Palmer's 1941 colour photos of a Youngstown open hearth; USGS 1970 aerial; postcards; modern CC photos.

**Sound:** original score, low brass and piano in C minor, lifting to A♭ in Part 9 and ending unresolved on the question; a wooden knock on every case card (no gavel);
courtroom room tone under parts 6–8; the Mill Town whistle (distant) at the open and the close. No Springsteen, no *Shout Youngstown*, no news footage.
