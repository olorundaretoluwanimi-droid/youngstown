**Title:** What Happened to Youngstown? The Day the Steel Mills Closed

**Description (first lines):**
On Black Monday, 19 September 1977, about 5,000 steelworkers in Youngstown, Ohio lost their jobs in a single day. Two years later, the town took U.S. Steel to federal court. This is that case, and you're the jury.
