# FACTS — What Happened to Youngstown? The Day the Steel Mills Closed

Every figure and quote in the script, with its source. Checked 27 Nov 2026. Where sources disagree, the choice is explained.

**Primary sources (public domain, U.S. court records):**
- **6th Cir.** — *Local 1330, United Steel Workers v. United States Steel Corp.*, 631 F.2d 1264 (6th Cir., 25 July 1980). Text: https://static.case.law/f2d/631/cases/1264-01.json (copy in research/f2d_631_cases_1264-01.txt)
- **N.D. Ohio** — same case, 492 F. Supp. 1 (N.D. Ohio 1980), Judge Thomas D. Lambros, No. C 79-2337 Y. Text: https://static.case.law/f-supp/492/cases/0001-01.json (research/f-supp_492_cases_0001-01.txt)
- **Lynd** — Staughton Lynd, *The Fight Against Shutdowns: Youngstown's Steel Mill Closings* (Singlejack Books, 1982). Lynd was the workers' lead lawyer. PDF: https://files.libcom.org/files/The%20Fight%20Against%20the%20Shutdowns.pdf (research/lynd.txt). Short quotes only.
